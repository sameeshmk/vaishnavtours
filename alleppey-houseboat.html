Before You Book

Important Houseboat Information

Availability

Availability can vary according to travel date, category, bedrooms and guest requirements.

Pricing

Prices displayed as “From” amounts are starting indications. Final rates may vary according to travel date, category, bedrooms and availability.

Booking Confirmation

A Check Availability enquiry does not confirm a booking. Confirmation follows agreement of the available option, final quotation and payment terms.

Special Requirements

Mention meal preferences, pickup, honeymoon decoration, celebrations, sightseeing or other requirements in the enquiry form.

Frequently Asked Questions

Alleppey Houseboat FAQs

How do I book an Alleppey houseboat?

Use the Check Availability form to provide your date, nights, guests, required bedrooms and preferred category. Vaishnav Tours will check available options and pricing.

Which houseboat categories can I request?

Premium, Deluxe, Luxury and Honeymoon houseboat options can be requested, subject to availability.

Can families book a private houseboat?

Yes. Enter the number of adults, children and bedrooms required so suitable private options can be checked.

Can couples request honeymoon houseboats?

Yes. Select Honeymoon Houseboat in the booking form and include any decoration, meal or celebration requirements under Special Requirements.

Are the website prices final?

Prices shown as “From” amounts are starting indications. Final pricing depends on travel dates, category, bedrooms, guests and availability.

Does an availability request confirm my booking?

No. The form is an enquiry. Vaishnav Tours will confirm available options and the final quotation before booking.
